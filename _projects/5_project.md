A **Fudan University – University of Turku** joint project on drone autonomy: get a Tello through a course of detection gates with no manual control.

- Computer-vision pipelines for **QR-code** and **color-based** gate detection
- Navigation logic that decides the next move from what the camera sees
- Flew the full course autonomously

**Stack:** Python, OpenCV, djitellopy
