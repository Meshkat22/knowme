An independent project: a DJI Tello drone that finds a human face and follows it around.

- Real-time face detection and tracking with **OpenCV** and **MediaPipe**
- Autonomous flight control through `djitellopy`
- Tuned the control loop so tracking stays smooth while both the drone and the person are moving

**Stack:** Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, djitellopy

**Demo:** [youtu.be/b6D0rk71ies](https://youtu.be/b6D0rk71ies)
